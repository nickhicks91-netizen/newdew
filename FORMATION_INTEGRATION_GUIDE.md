## 🔧 **Technical Considerations**

### **Performance Budget**
| Formation Type | Triangle Count | Update Frequency | GPU Load |
|----------------|----------------|------------------|----------|
| Sphere (current) | 2,048 | Static | Low |
| Rings | 10,000 | Static | Medium |
| Particle systems | 50,000 | 60 FPS | High |
| Field lines | 5,000 | Static | Low |
| Wave animation | 2,048 | 60 FPS | Medium |
| Heat maps | 0 (texture) | Static | Low |

### **Optimization Strategies**
1. **LOD (Level of Detail)**: Reduce geometry at distance
2. **Instancing**: Reuse geometry for particles
3. **GPU Particles**: Compute-shader based particles
4. **Texture Atlases**: Combine textures to reduce draws
5. **Frustum Culling**: Don't render off-screen objects
